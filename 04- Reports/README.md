# Reports and Ledger

## Introduction

MARG ERP provides different options for viewing business and accounting information.

Reports and ledger information help in monitoring transactions, balances and financial information.

## Ledger

A ledger is used to maintain financial information related to an account or party.

The Ledger screen can contain information such as:

- Account/Party Name
- Debit
- Credit
- Opening Balance
- Current Balance
- GSTN
- State
- Account Type

## Importance of Ledger

Ledger information helps in tracking financial transactions, payments, receipts and outstanding balances.
