# Billing, Sales and Purchase

## Introduction

MARG ERP provides facilities for recording and managing purchase and sales transactions.

Purchase transactions record goods received from suppliers, while sales transactions record goods supplied to customers.

## Purchase Management

The Purchase Alteration screen contains information such as:

- Supplier/Party Name
- Entry Number
- Date
- Product
- Packing
- Batch
- Quantity
- Free Quantity
- Purchase Rate
- Discount
- Amount
- GST
- Invoice Value

The Purchase Alteration option is used to view or modify an existing purchase transaction.

## Sales Management

The Sale Alteration screen contains information such as:

- Customer/Party Name
- Bill Number
- Date
- Product
- Packing
- Batch
- Quantity
- Free Quantity
- Sales Rate
- Discount
- Amount
- GST
- Invoice Value

The Sale Alteration option is used to view or modify an existing sales transaction.

## Purchase and Sales Flow

The basic flow can be understood as:

Purchase → Stock In

Sales → Stock Out

Purchase generally increases available stock, while sales generally decreases available stock.
