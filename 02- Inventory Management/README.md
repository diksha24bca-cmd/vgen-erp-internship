# Inventory Management

## Introduction

Inventory management involves maintaining and monitoring the stock of products in the business.

For a pharmaceutical business, inventory management is especially important because products have batch numbers and expiry dates.

## Expiry Stock

MARG ERP provides an Expiry Stock option to check expiry-related stock.

The screen provides options such as:

- Selected Company
- Stock As On Date
- Expired Stock
- Expiry Date
- Stock Status
- Selected Salt
- More Options

## Importance

Expiry stock management helps identify products that have expired or are approaching their expiry date.

This helps in maintaining proper stock control and reducing the risk of expired pharmaceutical products remaining in available stock.
