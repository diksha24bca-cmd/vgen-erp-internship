# Master Data

## Introduction

Master data contains the basic information that is used repeatedly during business transactions.

In MARG ERP, product and party-related information can be maintained through master records.

## Item Master

The Item Master is used to maintain information about products.

The item information can include:

- Product Name
- Packing
- Unit
- Stock
- Purchase Rate
- MRP
- Tax
- Company
- HSN/SAC
- Manufacturer
- Other product-related details

## Importance of Item Master

Maintaining correct item information is important because the product details are used during purchase, sales, billing and inventory management.
